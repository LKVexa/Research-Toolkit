# Acceptance plan — 0.1.0-rc.1

This is a plan, not a statement that checks have passed. Record actual commands, package/runtime hashes, outcomes, and limitations in release evidence. Freeze protocols and analytical expectations before running the methods. Retain unsuccessful and contradictory outcomes.

## R01 — scope and architecture

Verify the shared-foundation pin, source provenance, qualification records, and separate product/state roots. The runnable registry must include only qualified native fixtures and the authored numerical methods. Keep PC-Winter blocked and identify C0322-02 as duplicate evidence. Confirm that original notes cannot expand executable authority. Review the adapter contract, claim boundaries, preset definitions, and public/private report distinction before execution.

## R02 — protocol, data, and execution controls

Exercise strict JSON/schema handling, unknown-field and duplicate-key rejection, numeric bounds, minimum split sizes, unique row/group IDs, and prevention of cross-split group overlap. Show that requests contain final features without final labels or dataset metadata, and that the adapter rejects an injected final `y` field. Dataset registration must be content-addressed; changed data must not silently reuse a frozen protocol's digest.

Verify case-insensitive ID/version reservations, immutable protocol/run evidence, explicit supersession with reasons, and rejection of destinations overlapping protected roots. Test traversal, links/junctions, malformed state, incompatible state markers, existing destinations, missing parents, and concurrent reservation/locking where supported.

Verify execution timeout, output cap, process-count and memory controls, cancellation, and recovery. Resume must consume complete preserved outputs or mark the run interrupted, without silently restarting a missing process. No dataset or protocol may provide executable source, command-line fragments, or a network destination. Confirm that normal workflows use no model calls, downloads, or installation steps.

## R03 — independent comparison and truthful evidence

For each qualified native profile, compare raw observations against its frozen authored oracle and declared baseline. Validate both correct and deliberately faulty paths, including equivalent-baseline comparisons. Preserve native embedded-label disclosure.

For numerical profiles, check the prespecified exact linear, negative-slope, distribution-shift, zero-predictor, and same-baseline controls. Recompute MSE and MAE independently from persisted predictions and private final labels. Check paired-difference count, mean, population standard deviation, minimum, and maximum; confirm strict tolerance boundaries and no inferential statistics. Validate prediction ID coverage and rejection of non-finite or out-of-range outputs.

Run repeatability checks under unchanged frozen protocols. Identical deterministic repetitions must not be reported as additional independent samples. Do not choose a dataset, seed, tolerance, method, or stopping point after looking at final results. Investigate discrepancies as implementation failures or new versioned investigations, retaining the original evidence.

Check that the output separates `execution`, `implementation_checks`, `comparison`, and `source_claim`. Negative and inconclusive comparisons must remain visible. Report exact conditions and measured results without generalizing to papers or broad benchmarks.

## R04 — release, reproduction, and preservation

Check the final assembled package, its exact manifest, and required Windows/Python/NumPy/.NET environment. Test both launchers, paths with spaces, a relocated application, and the documented short-state-path workflow. Reject changed, missing, or unexpected files, including foundation, adapter, qualification, protocol, and dataset artifacts. Reject generated bytecode or credential/cache files from release assembly.

Verify stored evidence and compare runs without rerunning methods. Export aggregate JSON/HTML to a new destination with an existing parent. Inspect the actual export for excluded labels, predictions, per-row outcomes, and accidental private paths. Scan package and report artifacts for credential-shaped content without logging secret values.

Demonstrate rollback by renaming or moving only a disposable application copy inside the acceptance workspace, keeping state and source data unchanged. Recheck the original delivery/foundation/sibling-product inventories after acceptance. Publish gate decisions only after these checks complete against the exact released package.
